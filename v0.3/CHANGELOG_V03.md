# ASCENSION 0.3.0

- Added six timed contracts with committed capital, automatic settlement, safe/risky routes, shipment sizes and frozen quotes/outcomes. Risky shortcuts offer more coins but half the civic rewards, and can lose 15% of invested capital.
- Added three-round negotiations with visible agreement/tension effects, paid concessions, failure, cordial outcomes and full accords. Merchant, community and council relationships grant distinct production benefits; trust is capped at five per life.
- Added the Trade Clerk: optional, paid, per-route automatic reinvestment that also works offline and stops without debt.
- Exposed reputation/influence generation paths and prominent first-network/office purchase actions. Guildmaster needs reputation, not influence; the Rumour Network costs coins alone. Added passive milestone estimates with explicit action blockers.
- Finishing an ascension now returns to the top of Empire so the new district and resource guides are immediately visible.
- Added compact/expandable status, automatic collapse while scrolling, five bottom destinations, upgrade filters and payback sorting. All costs in income-recovery cards remain readable separately from action labels.
- Added generated atmospheric alley, harbour and citadel artwork, changing world cards and ascension imagery. Existing rank icons, typography and gold palette are retained.
- Added schema-2 activity persistence and a one-time original-save backup. Rank requirements, existing assets, policies and Legacy are preserved. Contract principal cannot inflate lifetime milestone totals.
- Strengthened regression coverage for actual contract settlement, failure, repetition, negotiation outcomes, JSON round trips, migration, offline rewards and narrow portrait interactions.

Later imperial/cosmic/divine eras and audio remain future work. Physical-device performance and OEM lifecycle behavior remain unverified. The Android emulator uses software graphics; cold-start times there do not predict phone loading times. See VALIDATION_V03.md for measured pacing and exact native evidence.
